Focus In Care Training & Consulting Inc. was built on a simple idea: providers deserve expert, personal guidance through a system that's easy to get lost in.

Portrait: Wanda Nitiss in the Focus In Care office
Our story

Founded by Wanda Nitiss

Wanda Nitiss founded Focus In Care after identifying a growing need for reliable, expert support in Florida's healthcare industry. Agencies were losing months to rejected applications, unclear requirements, and last-minute compliance scrambles.

With more than 20 years of experience providing quality improvement and quality assurance to healthcare providers, Wanda brings deep knowledge of the regulations, policies, and procedures that govern Florida providers — and she works directly with every client.

She is also a certified CPR, First Aid, and OSHA instructor, which means the same person who helps you get licensed can train the team that delivers your care.
