Courses

What we teach

CPR & AED

Proper CPR technique for adults, children, and infants, plus hands-on practice with AED trainers so you can act quickly and correctly in a cardiac emergency.

First Aid

Wound care, bandaging, choking relief, and recognizing the signs of common medical emergencies — the essentials for responding until help arrives.

OSHA Workplace Safety

Safety training that helps healthcare employers protect their staff and meet workplace safety requirements.

Agency In-Services

Custom in-service sessions for your team, designed around your services and your compliance needs.

Our approach

Learn by doing

Participants don't just watch — they practice. Every class includes guided, hands-on work so skills stick and confidence grows.

  • Instructor demonstrations, then supervised practice on manikins
  • AED trainers and realistic emergency scenarios
  • Small groups so everyone gets individual feedback
  • Certification card for participants who successfully complete the course
  • Classes available in English or Spanish
