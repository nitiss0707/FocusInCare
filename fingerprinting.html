Why it matters

Required for Florida healthcare workers

Florida requires a Level 2 background screening — which includes fingerprinting — for many healthcare owners, employees, and volunteers before they can work with patients or clients. Getting it right the first time keeps hiring and licensing on schedule.

We make the process simple: we explain what's required, capture your fingerprints accurately, and help employers make sure screenings are submitted correctly.

Photo: technician capturing a fingerprint on a Live Scan device
How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Schedule

    Call or message us to book a convenient time. Employers: make sure the screening has been initiated in the Care Provider Background Screening Clearinghouse first — we can walk you through it.

  2. Capture

    Bring a valid government-issued photo ID. Our Live Scan fingerprinting takes just a few minutes.

  3. Submit

    Your prints are transmitted electronically for processing, and results are made available to your employer or agency through the Clearinghouse.

FAQ

Fingerprinting questions

What do I need to bring?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license or passport). If your employer gave you any screening information or instructions, bring those too.

Does my employer need to do anything first?

For most healthcare positions, yes — the employer or agency initiates the screening in the Clearinghouse before you're fingerprinted. If you're unsure, call us and we'll help you check.

How long do results take?

Processing times are set by the state and federal agencies and can vary. Results are typically returned within a few business days and are shared with your employer or agency through the Clearinghouse.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend calling ahead so we can confirm your requirements and have you in and out quickly.
