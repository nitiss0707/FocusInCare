Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Focus In Care Training & Consulting Inc. ("Focus In Care," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through focusincaretc.com (the "Site") and how we use it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you contact us through the Site, by email, or by phone, we collect the details you choose to share — such as your name, organization, email address, phone number, the service you're interested in, and your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. If we use analytics tools, they collect similar usage data in aggregate.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations, classes, or appointments
  • To provide the services you request
  • To improve our Site and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Site or deliver our services (for example, website hosting or form-delivery services), with government agencies when you ask us to submit information on your behalf, or when required by law.

Sensitive information

Please do not send medical records or other sensitive health information through our website contact form. If sensitive documents are needed for your project, we'll arrange a secure way to exchange them.

Cookies

The Site itself does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and any analytics tools we add may set cookies to measure site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you provide. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you've shared with us by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.
