FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with new agencies or established ones?

Both. We help new owners through licensing and enrollment from the start, and we help established agencies with renewals, surveys, audits, policy updates, billing issues, and staff training.

Which Florida licenses do you help with?

We assist with AHCA licenses such as Nurse Registry, Home Health, and Homemaker & Companion services, as well as APD waiver provider enrollment. If you're unsure which license fits your business model, we'll help you decide during your consultation.

How long does the licensing process take?

It depends on the license type, how complete your documents are, and agency processing times. After your consultation we'll give you a realistic timeline for your specific situation.

Do you serve clients outside Miami?

Yes. Our office is in Miami, and our consulting services are available to providers throughout Florida. Training and fingerprinting are offered at our office or on-site for groups.

¿Ofrecen servicios en español?

Sí. Todos nuestros servicios de consultoría, capacitación y huellas digitales están disponibles en español. Ver el sitio en español.
