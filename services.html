FAQ

Questions about our consulting

What happens during the free consultation?

We learn about your organization, your goals, and any deadlines you're facing. You'll leave knowing which licenses or enrollments apply to you, the steps involved, and how we can help.

Can you help if we already applied and got stuck?

Yes. We can review an application in progress, respond to requests for additional information, and help you resolve deficiencies or delays.

How do you help with FLMMIS billing?

We help you understand FLMMIS billing requirements, review coding and documentation, and align your processes with Medicaid policy so claims go out accurately and payments arrive on time.

How do you keep us compliant after we're licensed?

Through policy and procedure updates, mock audits and quality reviews, annual review preparation, and staff training — whatever mix fits your organization.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of work. After your consultation we'll provide a clear quote so you know what to expect before any work begins.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us about your agency and we'll map out exactly what you need.
